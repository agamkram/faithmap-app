FaithMap

Two views. Mapped shows IRS-registered houses of worship with real map locations you can open in Maps. Census shows exactly the 2020 U.S. Religion Census congregation count for each county and religion: real Mapped places first, then anonymous dots for any shortfall. Those extras have no street address. Where Mapped has more places than the census counted, the extras stay in Mapped view only — so in Census view the pin count matches the total.
