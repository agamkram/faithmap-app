FaithMap

Two views. Mapped is buildings you can open in Maps. The base is HIFLD (DHS): IRS 501(c)(3) mailing addresses, filled with OpenStreetMap houses of worship. Mosques come from MosqueIndex so Muslim counts sit next to the U.S. Mosque Survey.

Census is the 2020 U.S. Religion Census (ASARB): that congregation count per county and religion, Mapped pins first, then anonymous dots for any shortfall. Those extras have no address. Where Mapped has more than the census, the extras stay in Mapped only.
